Estimate from weighed components

Quantity times measured unit weight creates each component category total, and the selected packaging allowance is applied after the component sum. Use grams; do not treat the shipping-weight estimate as a carrier quote or dimensional-weight calculation.

Use the result in a design decision

Weigh a representative deck, punchboard, wooden piece, and rulebook when possible. A heavy box or insert can shift the final result enough to require a new freight and fulfillment scenario.
