A connected workflow

Plan → acquire → diagnose → compare → pace

  1. Keep audience sources separate and remove known overlap.
  2. Log actual acquisition and spend on a consistent schedule.
  3. Use channel stages to locate a measurable leak before changing creative or spend.
  4. Compare attributed sources with the costs you can defend.
  5. During the live campaign, replace launch assumptions with actual cumulative records.

What these tools do not decide

Tracking is incomplete by nature.

Referral tags, analytics blockers, cross-device journeys, audience overlap, cancellations, and later delivery costs can change the picture. These tools organize creator-entered evidence; they do not predict success, guarantee funding, or replace platform analytics.

Before launch

Use ranges instead of one assumed conversion rate, then log actual growth by source.

During launch

Separate funnel-rate diagnosis from referral economics so a cheap click is not mistaken for a useful backer.

During the campaign

Compare recent entered pace with the run rate needed for the remaining days; do not present a linear extension as probability.
