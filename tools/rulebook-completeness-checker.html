Draft review

Rate the text a new reader has now: Clear means the rule is explicit and usable without spoken help. Unclear means it exists but leaves a choice, timing, term, or exception open. Missing means the reader cannot complete that part from the packet.

Do not rate what you plan to add. The result prioritizes missing sections before unclear ones.

What this rulebook checklist tests

A complete packet lets a first-time group identify the game objective, inventory the components, build the starting state, take legal turns, track progress, and finish and resolve a tie. It also gives readers consistent terms, representative examples, and enough exception handling to continue without the designer.

Completeness and clarity are different tests. A draft can contain every heading and still leave readers unsure about timing or permissions. A short prototype packet can be testable if it clearly supports setup, action, resolution, and ending. Mark only what the current draft supports.

Worked review example

Suppose setup is absent and the tie rule says only “most points wins.” Mark Setup as Missing and Ties as Unclear. The checker puts setup first, recommends a numbered sequence with the final ready-to-play state, and then asks for a complete tiebreak sequence. That is more actionable than a single completeness score.

How to interpret the result

Incomplete means at least one section is missing; finish those instructions before blind testing. Needs clarity pass means all sections exist but at least one requires a narrow reader test. Test-ready draft means this self-screen found no gap—not that the rules are proven understandable.

Turn a review into evidence

After revising, give the packet to a fresh group without teaching. Log their first questions, pauses, rule lookups, disagreements, and any place play stops. If readers repeatedly stop on a section marked clear, revise that exact passage and test it again. The Playtesting & Rulebook Validation workflow connects this review to packet readiness and issue prioritization.
