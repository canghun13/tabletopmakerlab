Completeness and clarity are different tests

A rulebook can include every heading and still leave new readers unsure what they may do. Conversely, a short prototype packet can be testable if it clearly lets players set up, choose actions, resolve consequences, and end the game. Mark only what the draft currently supports.

Turn a review into evidence

Fix missing setup, turn structure, legal actions, scoring, and end condition before a broad blind test. Then give a fresh group the packet and log their actual questions. If players repeatedly stop on a clear-marked passage, revise it and retest that exact situation rather than assuming the problem is player skill.
