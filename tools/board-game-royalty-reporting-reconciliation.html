Cluster boundary

Actual-period controls, not deal advice.

Every rate, base, deduction, reserve percentage, and deadline comes from the user. The tools organize arithmetic and timing evidence; they do not interpret a contract.

Use this cluster after statements begin.

The six tools here work with actual period rows, reported amounts, cumulative unit bands, reserve movements, and dates. They do not supply market rates or decide what a party owes.

Minimum evidence set

Keep the source beside the calculation.

  • The signed agreement and any amendments
  • The publisher or licensee statement for the same period
  • Sales, returns, sublicense, and payment records used to build it
  • A note explaining any creator-entered deduction, rate, or deadline

Important limit

A variance is a question, not an accusation.

Timing differences, later returns, currency conversion, tax treatment, contract definitions, and omitted context can all explain a mismatch. Confirm the source and seek qualified legal or accounting help when the consequence matters.
