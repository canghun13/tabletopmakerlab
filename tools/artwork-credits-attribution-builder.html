Separate the ledger from the rendered credits

The ledger preserves why a line exists and which asset it covers. The generated block uses only the exact approved credit text, so a publication edit does not silently erase the source or rights record.

Rights basis is broader than an open license

A project may rely on commissioned-work terms, an assignment, an employee agreement, a stock license, public-domain evidence, or an open license. Record the basis you actually reviewed; this builder does not decide ownership or compatibility.
