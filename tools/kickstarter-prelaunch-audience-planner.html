Why use conversion ranges?

One assumed conversion rate can hide how sensitive your launch plan is. A low/high range keeps that sensitivity visible while the audience is still being built.

Avoid double counting

Email subscribers may also follow the Kickstarter prelaunch page or hold a reservation. Use mutually exclusive rows where possible, or reduce overlapping counts before reading the total.
