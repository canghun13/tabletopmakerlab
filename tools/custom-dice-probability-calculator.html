Calculation method

Every listed entry is an equally likely physical face, including repeated scores. The calculation counts all ordered roll outcomes for independent dice with the same face list. For Exact target, add the full counts of totals equal to the target. For At least target, add the counts of totals greater than or equal to it. Divide that favorable count by the face count raised to the number of dice. Expected values use the exact integer sum of face scores before conversion to display averages; probabilities and averages are rounded for display.

Assumptions and limits

This is a tabletop design scenario using your inputs, not a prediction of player experience. Check your game's exact rules and edge cases before finalizing a design. Dice are independent and each physical face is equally likely. This does not model weighted physical dice, exploding dice, rerolls or keep-highest rules.

Use 1–30 integer face scores, 1–20 dice and a non-negative integer target. Signed face scores are allowed. Decimal scores must first be scaled to integers together with the target, avoiding floating-point equality ambiguity. Counts and possible pool totals must stay within JavaScript’s safe integer range. Distributions exceeding 50,000 distinct totals or 2,000,000 transition steps are rejected to bound browser work. These are computational limits, not game-design recommendations.

Check a weighted example

For one die with faces 1, 1, 2, exactly 1 has two favorable physical outcomes out of three: 66.67%. For two ordinary d6, exactly 7 has six ordered outcomes out of 36: 16.67%. At least 7 instead includes 21 outcomes out of 36: 58.33%. Expected values are averages, not target probabilities.

Use the result in a design decision

A die with two success symbols and four non-success faces has a different success chance from a die with six distinct labels. Use the output to test whether a custom icon distribution communicates the intended risk to players.
