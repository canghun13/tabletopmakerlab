Why gameplay context belongs in the brief

A beautiful image can still fail its component if it suggests the wrong role, hides under card text, or loses its distinguishing features at final size. The brief connects the visual request to the player-facing job.

What to confirm outside this builder

Agree on rights, fees, revision rounds, kill fees, deadlines, portfolio permissions, generative-AI policy, source files, and acceptance terms in the actual contract. Confirm bleed, color space, file formats, and naming with the production partner.
