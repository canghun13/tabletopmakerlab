Suggested sequence

Stable IDs connect the work.

Use the same asset ID in the brief, tracker, delivery manifest, and credit ledger. A filename may change during revision; the stable ID preserves the project decision trail.

Deliberate boundary

Evidence, not automatic approval.

These tools do not evaluate artistic quality, contracts, ownership, color profiles, layered files, or a manufacturer’s unpublished rules. Record the real partner requirements and make the final approval yourself.
