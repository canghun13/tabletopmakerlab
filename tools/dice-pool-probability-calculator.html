Calculation method

Each fair die has integer faces 1 through s. A face at least the success threshold is one success; dice are independent and identical. The per-die chance p is the number of successful faces divided by s. Exactly k successes among d dice has probability C(d,k) p^k (1 − p)^(d − k); at least k sums that event through d. Expected successes are d × p. The extra-die comparison uses the same selected event, not a different target mode.

Assumptions and limits

This is a tabletop design scenario using your inputs, not a prediction of player experience. Check your game's exact rules and edge cases before finalizing a design. Use safe whole-number inputs: 0–1,000 dice, at least one side per die, and non-negative thresholds and required counts. The dice cap is a browser computation limit, not a recommended game-design range. Decimal inputs are rejected, not rounded or interpreted as partial dice or faces. Weighted or mixed dice, rerolls and correlated results need a different model. Probabilities use floating-point arithmetic and are rounded for display.

Separate die chance from pool chance

Three d6 with a success threshold of 5 have p = 2/6. At least one success is 70.37%, but exactly one is 44.44%; expected successes are 1. These are different events. The outside-event readout is the complement of the selected event, not always the chance of zero successes.

Use the result in a design decision

Compare at least one success with two or more before adding dice or lowering a threshold. A threshold above all faces gives p = 0; a threshold of 0 or 1 gives p = 1. With zero dice, exactly zero and at least zero successes have probability 100%; positive required counts have probability 0%. A required count above the pool is a valid impossible event, not an input error. The possible range excludes unattainable counts when p is 0 or 1. At 1,000 dice the extra-die comparison is omitted.
