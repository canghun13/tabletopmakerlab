Board Game Manufacturing Glossary

Working terms for production quotes and inbound planning.

Use terms with their project context

Manufacturing vocabulary becomes useful when it identifies a decision: MOQ affects order quantity, tooling may be a fixed setup cost, dielines describe a cut or fold layout, and fulfillment begins after production rather than at factory completion. Record the definition used in a specific quote because suppliers can use shorthand differently.

How to apply it

When a quote says FOB, inspection, or assembly, ask what work, location, and document are included. A glossary term is a prompt for a clarification, not evidence that a cost or responsibility has been included.

Questions and decisions

Terms can change meaning across regions, contracts, and production methods. Confirm the supplier’s written scope and the governing agreement before budgeting or making a delivery promise.
